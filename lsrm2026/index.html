About the Conference

The International Conference LSRM-2026 aims to foster research from diverse cultural backgrounds, welcoming participation from educators, scholars, industrialists, and policymakers. In alignment with NEP 2020, it provides a platform for interdisciplinary discussions on the Indian Knowledge System.
